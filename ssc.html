Supreme Student Council

The Voice of the Student Body The Supreme Student Council (SSC) is the highest governing body for students at Misamis Union Educational Foundation INC. (MUEFI). We are a team of elected student leaders dedicated to representing your interests, protecting your rights, and serving as the vital bridge between the studentry and the school administration.
